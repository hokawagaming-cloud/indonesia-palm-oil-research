# Research Context

_Last updated: 2026-11-24_

## Objective

Identify underappreciated investment opportunities in Indonesian palm oil equities before fundamental catalysts are fully priced in, combining fundamental and quantitative research (quantamental).

## Scope

- **Market:** Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX), plus Indonesian palm oil exposure listed elsewhere if relevant (to be decided).
- **Sector:** Upstream plantations (primary), integrated upstream/downstream producers, and possibly downstream refiners and biodiesel names.
- **Horizon:** To be defined (see Open questions).

## Key research questions

1. Which fundamental drivers (CPO price, production growth, tree age profile, cost per tonne, export levy/duty, biodiesel mandate, FX) explain the cross-section and time series of plantation equity returns?
2. Where is the market mispricing these drivers, e.g. production recovery from replanting, cost deflation, or mandate-driven demand?
3. Which upcoming catalysts (B40/B50 mandate changes, levy revisions, EUDR enforcement, El Niño/La Niña cycles, monthly production releases, earnings) are not yet reflected in prices or consensus?
4. Can quantitative signals (valuation, momentum, revisions, production surprises, weather) generate risk-adjusted excess returns out of sample?

## Approach (planned, not started)

1. **Macro** — CPO supply/demand, prices, policy, weather.
2. **Industry** — structure, costs, yields, regulation, peer benchmarks.
3. **Company** — operating data, financials, management, governance, valuation.
4. **Quant** — data pipeline, signal design, point-in-time backtests.
5. **Synthesis** — rank opportunities, define catalysts, write theses.
