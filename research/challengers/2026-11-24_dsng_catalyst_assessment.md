# DSNG — Shipment Catch-up / Inventory Monetisation Catalyst vs TAPG (Phase III, P6)

## Evidence

- **Inventory (S-041, primary, verified):** CPO inventory ~23kt (end-Jun-26) → 52kt (end-Sep-26) because of shipment delays and vessel congestion; Q3 CPO sales −20.4% QoQ. Company volumes give a consistent Q3 build of +25kt (production 152kt vs sales 127kt, DER).
- **Profit to owners (9M26 FS p7, OBS):** 1,465.0bn (9M25: 1,316.9bn).
- **Quarterly profit to owners (S-020-style Yahoo, secondary):** Q4'25 523.6bn; Q1'26 430.4bn; Q2'26 571.0bn; Q3'26 463.6bn.
- **Consensus (S&P via Yahoo, 4 analysts):**
  - FY26 EPS 213.31 (range 181.4–274.9); 7 days ago it was 219.06 (one downward revision in the last 7 days).
  - Shares 10,599.8mn, so FY26 profit ≈ 2,261bn and **implied Q4 ≈ 796bn** (range ≈ 458–1,449).
  - FY27 EPS 220.4 (+3%).
- **Indicative release effect (DER/EST):** 29kt × IDR 15,520/kg ≈ IDR 450bn of revenue. At the 9M EBITDA margin (32%, S-041) that is ≈ 144bn EBITDA, or ≈ 110bn after tax. A normal quarter (Q1–Q3 2026 average ≈ 488bn) plus the release gives **≈ 600bn**, below the consensus-implied 796bn.

  - Price IDR 1,275, i.e. 6.0× FY26 consensus EPS.

## Verdict

- The DSNG catalyst is **real and well evidenced**, but it **does not appear to be an expectations gap**: consensus-implied Q4 is already above our rough release-inclusive estimate.
- TAPG has the larger Q4 gap but weaker evidence and an unvalidated model.
- **TAPG remains primary coverage.**
- Both stocks' post-9M underperformance looks sector- or style-related (both are about −13% vs the remaining peers); that remains a hypothesis.
