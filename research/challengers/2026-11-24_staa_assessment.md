## Verdict

- STAA has the clearest **operating-change story** (the downstream ramp), and it trades at the **lowest multiple**.
- But the expectations evidence is the weakest of the three (one analyst, no PIT history), refinery economics are unverifiable without capacity and FS data, and liquidity is thin.
- Under the uniform valuation rule, expected return is ≈ +7.5%, below the hurdle.
- **Not a trade.** To revisit: refinery capacity and utilisation, FS cash conversion, a second consensus source.
