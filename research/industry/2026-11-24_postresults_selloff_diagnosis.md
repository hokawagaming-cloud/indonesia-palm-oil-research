## 2. Candidate causes

| Cause | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Commodity repricing** | Brent −24% (USD 117 → 88), weakening the biodiesel/POGO link; KPBN CPO tenders −3.8% Oct→Nov (secondary, few observations); WB palm oil Oct −5% m/m. The November export-charge cut (−USD 62/t) cushioned the reference netback (−1.3 USD/t). | Real and sector-wide, but it does **not** explain why LSIP/SSMS/SIMP rose. It is a background headwind. |
| **Regulatory risk** | The government is reviewing B50 (Katadata; ESDM "evaluating B50"). There is talk of raising the levy to fund B50. Earlier in the year (May) a rumoured state export agency hit DSNG/TAPG ~15% in a day (Kontan). | Plausible sector overhang. It is not name-specific, and there is no dated new decree in the window. |
| **Foreign flows** | No primary data available (IDX foreign net buy/sell not accessible; S-013 blocked). TAPG free float is only ~17% (broker reports). | **Unresolved**, a data gap. |
| **Liquidity** | Traded value 29 Oct–24 Nov vs Jul–Sep average: TAPG 0.38×, DSNG 0.50×, STAA 0.32×, SSMS 0.37×. Only AALI (1.56×) and SMAR (2.04×) rose. | The moves happened on **thin volume**. That is consistent with low-conviction drift, not forced selling. Low liquidity also amplifies small flows. |
| **Earnings expectations** | Rank correlation between 30-day FY26 EPS revisions and returns across the 7 names with data ≈ 0 (n = 7). TAPG fell despite a small upgrade; DSNG fell with a small downgrade. | Revisions do not explain the cross-section. Name-specific: TAPG's FY27 guidance (El Niño volume −5% to −10%, fertiliser +25%) and DSNG's lower FFB plus inventory, i.e. **FY27 expectations**, are the most consistent explanation. That remains a hypothesis. |

## 3. Conclusion

- The TAPG/DSNG weakness is best described as **name-specific FY27 concern on thin liquidity, against a commodity headwind** (Brent, B50 review).
- It is not a sector-wide de-rating, and the evidence does not support reading it as mispricing.
- TAPG's ex-dividend date (11 Nov, IDR 70) is already removed by using adjusted prices.
