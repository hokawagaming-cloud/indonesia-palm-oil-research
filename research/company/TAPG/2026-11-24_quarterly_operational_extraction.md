## Scope (verified)

- **Production (FFB, processed, CPO, PK, OER, KER) is group-wide, i.e. it includes associates.** KBV's FY2025 quarterly sums match the AR2025 totals: nucleus 3,040 = 3,040; plasma 384 = 384; CPO 952 vs 953; PK 202 = 202; processed 4,090 vs 4,089. Aldiracita states it explicitly: "Production figures include the associate company".
- **Sales volumes, ASP, fertiliser and purchased-FFB costs are consolidated.** Check: CPO revenue = volume × ASP holds for 9M26 (507.8kt × 15,157 = 7,697bn vs 7,700bn) and for 3Q26 (2,918bn vs 2,920bn).
- **The consolidated share of production is now known annually.** The Public Expose splits FY2021–25 into subsidiaries vs associates. FY2025 subsidiary CPO was 652kt (68% of the 953kt group total), against consolidated CPO sales of 658.9kt; the difference is purchased CPO and inventory. Every FY2021–25 split sums to the AR totals (PASS). This **closes gap G11 at annual frequency**. Quarterly splits are not disclosed.

## Discrepancies

1. **Q1 2026 revision.**
   - First reported (press 28 Apr 2026; KBV chart): nucleus FFB 698kt, CPO 227kt, OER 24.1%.
   - Implied by the 9M26 cumulative totals (newsletter Vol 24 via ALD; KBV's own text agrees): nucleus 716.2kt, plasma 80.9kt, CPO 214.8kt, OER about 22.7%.
   - So KBV's quarterly charts do not sum to KBV's own 9M text.
   - **Unexplained.** Treat Q1 2026 as revised. Before 28–29 Oct 2026, only the first-reported value is point-in-time.
2. **Processed-FFB split.**
   - KBV's "internal (nucleus+plasma) / external" split does not match the company's definitions. FY2025 KBV internal is 2,833 vs company internal 3,031 (= AR "nucleus processed"); company external is 1,058 (= plasma 400 + third party 659).
   - Aldiracita's "external" (processed − nucleus − plasma; 230kt in 3Q26) is a third definition.
   - Totals agree, so only totals should be used, and the split should come from the AR/Public Expose (D-012).
3. **Rounding.** 3Q25 plasma (90 vs 90.6) and 3Q26 KER (KBV chart 5.3% vs text and ALD 5.4%) differ only by rounding.
4. **KBV Exhibit 2 errors.**
   - It prints 3Q26 net profit as 1,096bn; its own text and the filing give 1,465bn.
   - Its QoQ/YoY column headers are swapped.
   - Its EBITDA definition differs from Aldiracita's (1,996 vs 1,632bn).
5. **Indo Premier (7 Oct, pre-results)** put Q3 sales volume at "~245k tons", vs KBV's 187.5kt CPO. This is probably a different product scope (e.g. CPO+PK+PKO). Not reconciled.
6. **Ajaib's FY2023 ASP chart** shows CPO 7,697 / PK 3,360 / PKO 12,236 IDR/kg, vs the AR2024 figures of 11,115 / 5,392 / 10,985. That is a broker error; Ajaib is not used for ASP history.
7. **Public Expose p9** prints FY2025 CPO as 937kt in one chart vs 953kt elsewhere in the same deck and in the AR. An internal inconsistency in the deck.

## Point-in-time availability (D-011)

- The historical quarters in KBV/ALD were **first seen in documents dated 29 Oct 2026**. The original newsletters (S-033) were not obtained, so their disclosure dates are **unverified**. Backtests must not use any quarterly operating figure before its `first_seen` date unless a dated original is found.
- 1Q25 and 1Q26 nucleus FFB were public by 28 Apr 2026 (Bareksa).
- The FY2021–25 subsidiary/associate split was public from the IDX filing on 16 Mar 2026, 21:12 WIB.
- **Financial-statement filings now have IDX timestamps** from the S-027 mirror for H1 2023 to 9M 2026 and for the annual reports FY2023–25 (`TAPG_disclosure_dates.csv`). Rule A-018: a filing before 09:00 WIB is usable the same trading day; otherwise from the next trading day.
