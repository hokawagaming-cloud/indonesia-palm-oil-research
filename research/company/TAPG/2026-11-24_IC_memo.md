# Investment Committee Memo — TAPG (Triputra Agro Persada), Phase III

_Date: 2026-11-24 · Status: RESEARCH, no recommendation · Price IDR 1,710 (24 Nov) · All forecasts exploratory (not validated; PIT test n = 2)_

## 1. What does the market expect?

- **Consensus** (S&P Global via Yahoo/StockAnalysis, 4 analysts, 24 Nov; the same dataset, not two sources):
  - FY26 profit ≈ **IDR 4,220bn** (EPS 212.9).
  - FY27 ≈ **4,473bn** (EPS 225.3, +6%).
  - The FY26 number was cut before the 9M results (215.5 → 208.1) and raised once since (→ 212.9). FY27 has been rising.
  - EPS is labelled "non-GAAP adjusted", but the FY25 base equals reported EPS (186), so the basis is unreconciled.
- **Implied Q4 2026:** about **714–720bn**, vs 1,023bn in Q4 2025 and 1,465bn in Q3 2026. The older Bloomberg (29 Oct) and KB Valbury figures imply about 612–616bn and are stale or under review.
- **Implied by the share price:** at 8.0× FY26 / 7.6× FY27, the price capitalises about **4.1tn of sustainable profit** (ke 12.5%, g 2%). That is roughly current earnings, and about 46% above the FY2023–25 average.

## 2. What do we expect?

| IDR bn | Bear | Base | Bull | Consensus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 2026 profit to owners | 765 | **1,209** | 1,651 | ≈ 714–720 |
| FY2026 | 4,270 | **4,715** | 5,157 | 4,220 |
| FY2027 | 3,638 | **4,062** | 4,969 | 4,473 |

The base Q4 was revised from 1,268bn: other operating items are now anchored to the Q4 2025 year-end level of −72bn instead of Q3's +6bn (D-018).

**Hypothesis:** FY26 about +12% and Q4 about +69% above consensus. FY27 about **−9% below** consensus.

## 3. Why are our expectations different?

**Q4 2026 (we are above consensus):**
1. Consensus implies a Q4 about 30% below Q4 2025. In our cost structure, that needs several adverse drivers at once:
   - Joint grid: no combination without an extreme driver reaches 720bn.
   - All drivers "stressed" together gives about 728bn.
   - The fertiliser catch-up alone would need about 1.0tn (Q3 was 75bn).
2. An unsold finished-goods build is likely to be sold. It grew IDR 288bn → 525bn → 664bn (Dec → Jun → Sep, OBS).
3. The November export-charge cut (−USD 62/t) offsets 98% of the reference-price fall on the regulatory basis. However, KPBN tenders still fell about 4% in Oct→Nov.

**FY2027 (we are below consensus):**
- Volume: 2026 JJAS rainfall was −65% vs normal in Central Kalimantan, a deeper deficit than 2015. ONI is 2.46. Management guides FY27 production −5% to −10% (via Indo Premier).
- Cost: fertiliser +25% guided.
- Our base has FY27 −14% vs our FY26. Consensus has +6%.

## 4. What could prove us wrong?

- **Unknown fertiliser level.** Only Q3 2026 (75bn) and YoY changes are disclosed; FY25 (~800bn) is a broker estimate. A larger Q4 catch-up, or pre-booking of FY27 fertiliser, would move Q4 toward consensus.
- **ASP.** A −10% to −15% Q4 ASP vs Q3 costs 100–215bn. KPBN Nov-to-date is about −8% vs the Q3 average.
- **Q4 one-offs.** USTP (JV) also bears fertiliser catch-up; year-end biological-asset fair value changes, impairments and tax adjustments.
- **Model risk.**
  - The structure is calibrated on one quarter (3Q26) of broker-reported inputs.
  - There is no historical PIT validation (n = 2; naive benchmarks err by 11–43%).
  - The Q1 2026 operating data were revised after first publication.
- **On FY27 we may be too bearish:** El Niño impacts have been small for TAPG before (2024 −3.4%), and the CPO price could rise on El Niño supply loss.

## 5. Is the difference already priced in?

- **The Q4 beat is not in consensus, but it barely matters for value.** It is about one quarter (≈ +0.5tn, partly timing), so it moves normalised value by low single digits.
- **The price already assumes about 4.1tn of sustainable profit.** That equals our FY27 base, so on our FY27 view there is no undervaluation.
- **Post-results behaviour:**
  - No reaction on the day (CAR [0,1] ≈ 0, volume 0.7×), then −13% vs IHSG and −15% vs peers by 24 Nov.
  - DSNG shows the same pattern (−13% to −14% after its 9M), which points to sector or style factors rather than TAPG news.
  - The market may already be looking through FY26 strength to FY27, i.e. it is not extrapolating. Underperformance is **not** evidence of undervaluation.

## 6. Potential risk-adjusted return (12 months, indicative)

Value is scaled to sustainable earnings (reverse-valuation central case), plus about 12 months of dividends. Dividends paid in 2026 were IDR 221/share (91 + 60 + 70), a 12.9% trailing yield; we assume IDR 160–230 forward.

| Case | Sustainable profit | Price change | Dividends | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | mean-reversion to the FY2023–25 average, 2.8tn | ≈ −32% | +9% | **≈ −23%** |
| Base | our FY27 base, 4.06tn | ≈ −1% | +11% | **≈ +10%** |
| Bull | our FY27 bull, 4.97tn | ≈ +21% | +13% | **≈ +34%** |

Upside/downside is about 1.5:1. The base return is almost entirely dividend carry. The edge rests on an unvalidated model and on a near-term catalyst that does not change normalised value.

## Conclusion

- **No BUY.** The expectations gap is real for Q4 2026, but it is (a) small in value terms, (b) offset by our below-consensus FY27 view, and (c) not validated.
- **TAPG stays primary coverage on watch.**
- DSNG has better-evidenced inventory monetisation, but consensus already implies a strong Q4 (≈ 796bn vs our rough ≈ 600bn), so it is not a superior expectations opportunity.
