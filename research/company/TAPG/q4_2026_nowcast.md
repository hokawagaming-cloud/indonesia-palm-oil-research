## Can the implied ~612bn Q4 be justified?

- Holding the other base inputs, Q4 fertiliser would need to be about **1,198bn** (vs 75bn in Q3 2026) to bring Q4 profit to 612bn.
- Alternatively, CPO ASP would need to fall about **-43%** vs Q3 with base fertiliser (230bn).
- Neither is ruled out by the data in hand. Fertiliser levels are known only for Q3 2026 (75bn) and as YoY changes (9M −23%). One broker estimated FY24 fertiliser at about 790bn (Phintraco, secondary), and management guides 2027 fertiliser +25% YoY (Indo Premier, 7 Oct). So the plausible size of the catch-up is uncertain.
- The implied ~612bn requires a combination of a large fertiliser catch-up, falling CPO prices and lower JV income. The base case here (about 1,341bn) sits between the KBV/consensus-implied figure and Indo Premier's pre-results figure.
