# LSIP — Executive Investment Summary (2026-11-26)

**HOLD / WATCH** · Price IDR 1,575 · Fair value IDR 1,203 / **1,790** / 2,491 (bear/base/bull) · 12m target IDR 1,904 · BUY at ≤ IDR 1,473 or on verified capital return.

- **Question:** is LSIP's IDR 8,516bn cash genuinely excess, or needed to regenerate an ageing estate? **Answer: it is genuinely excess** — even the sustainable replanting programme (scenario B, ~3,573 ha/yr) needs IDR 401-557bn of replanting capex a year (nominal, 2030-2040), fully funded from operating cash flow. Distributable surplus is IDR 6,863bn (1,006/share). The problem is access, not need.
- **Ageing is real but not the main value driver.** >20-year palms were 41% of the estate at FY2023 (last disclosure); replanting runs at ~1,839 ha/yr vs 3,573 needed. Yields fell to 13.4 t/ha (TAPG 24.3); age explains ~34% of the gap. Accelerating replanting adds only ~IDR 69/share at LSIP's productivity.
- **Capital allocation is the binding constraint.** 30% payout leaves minorities a cash-flow value of IDR 537/share vs intrinsic IDR 2,034; 100% payout would lift it to IDR 1,521. The market credits ~62% of surplus cash at our base operations.
- **Versus TAPG:** LSIP base return 27% vs TAPG -6%; bear -14% vs -31%. TAPG has far better plantation economics, but its price capitalises ~IDR 4,278bn of sustainable profit. **LSIP offers the better risk-adjusted return, conditional on cash realisation ≥ ~69%.**
- **Earnings risk:** our FY2027E profit 1,609bn is -27% vs consensus 2,210bn; Q4 2026E 534bn vs consensus-implied 360bn (timing item). Unvalidated models.
- **Catalysts:** buyback report (Jan 2027), FY2026 results (Feb 2027), AGM payout (Jun 2027).
- **Invalidation:** payout cut or more related-party deposits; structural yield decline with fertiliser restored; long-run CPO < IDR 12,000/kg.
